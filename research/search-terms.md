# Search terms and content opportunities

Terrarium storefront: https://store.terrariumproject.org
Repo: AQuantumArchitect/terrarium-storefront
Researched: 11 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles
Voice to keep: Suburban Reality. Neighbors coordinating, not survivalist hype.

This note maps preparedness and resilience search intent that big prepper sites underserve, especially suburban, family, neighborhood, renter, and California-hazard angles. It is a content brief, not a media plan to post from.

## Method and limits

Sources used this turn:

- Live sitemap and page titles/descriptions fetched from https://store.terrariumproject.org/sitemap.xml and key URLs on 11 November 2026.
- Google Autocomplete via the public suggest API (`suggestqueries.google.com`, client=firefox) for ~50 seed phrases, same day.
- Web search of official guidance (Ready.gov, CDC, USDA/FoodSafety.gov, EPA, Cal OES, SCE/PSPS pages, Map Your Neighborhood municipal materials), Reddit threads in r/preppers (and related BIFL/homestead discussion), and public Pinterest preparedness boards.

**No search volumes are reported.** This run did not have Google Keyword Planner, Semrush, Ahrefs, or similar. Ranking is qualitative. When a query is called "high," "medium," or "niche," that means:

- **Autocomplete presence:** does the phrase complete, and how tight is the suggestion cluster?
- **SERP shape:** who occupies the first results (Ready.gov / CDC / utilities vs. municipal PDFs vs. big prepper blogs vs. thin affiliate lists vs. Reddit)?
- **Forum demand:** recurring Reddit and community questions, especially "I don't want to sound like a prepper" and suburban/renter constraints.
- **Fit:** does the query match Terrarium's neighborhood-coordination voice, or does it pull toward bunker / bug-out / doomsday language?

Treat the ranked list as a working order for pages and pins, not as a traffic forecast.

### What the SERPs generally look like

Head terms such as "72 hour kit," "how much water to store," "family emergency plan," and "CERT training near me" are real demand and also crowded. Ready.gov, Red Cross, CDC, USDA, and utility or city pages take the informational slots. Big prepper and food-storage blogs (Food Storage Moms and similar) take the commercial listicles. That is not a reason to ignore those queries, but it is a reason to win on a distinct angle: suburban household, renters, a street of neighbors, California hazards, no bunker language.

The clearer openings are queries official sites answer incompletely or in PDF form, and that prepper sites answer with the wrong voice: Map Your Neighborhood, talking to neighbors without scaring them, PSPS as a planned shutoff rather than a grid collapse, apartment water storage, boil-water notices, toilets when the municipal supply is off, wildfire smoke indoor air, Zone 0, GMRS vs ham for a block, and hosting evacuees.

One autocomplete caution: the acronym **PSPS** is polluted by the "pspsps" cat meme. Use the full phrase **public safety power shutoff** in titles and headings. **Neighborhood** autocomplete is dominated by **neighborhood watch** (crime). Do not compete for "how to start a neighborhood watch." Use "neighborhood emergency preparedness," "Map Your Neighborhood," and "get to know your neighbors."

## 1. Top 30 target queries

Intent labels: **I** = informational, **C** = commercial investigation (what to get), **L** = local ("near me"), **A** = action / how-to.

Competition is a read of who already ranks, not a difficulty score.

### Highest-fit openings (start here)

**1. neighborhood emergency preparedness**
- Intent: I / A. People looking past household kits toward the street.
- Page: `/coordination`, `/neighbors`, `/blog/first-10-steps-neighborhood-coordination`. Strengthen these; do not create a competing URL.
- Competition: Medium. Extension services, HSToday-style essays, and municipal "prepare your neighborhood" pages (Seattle, Pasadena, Mercer Island) occupy the term. Few stores speak in a calm suburban voice with a clear next step.
- Evidence: Autocomplete cluster under "neighborhood emergency" includes "neighborhood emergency preparedness," "neighborhood emergency team," and "neighborhood emergency response team." Official and research pieces increasingly argue household kits are not enough (HSToday; MU Extension DM502).

**2. Map Your Neighborhood program**
- Intent: I / A. People have heard of MYN and want the 9 steps, a workbook, or how to host a meeting.
- Page: `/product/map-your-neighborhood-block`, `/blog/map-your-street-in-an-afternoon`, `/civic`. A dedicated plain-language MYN explainer would still help; treat it as a new article that funnels to the existing product and blog.
- Competition: Medium-low commercially. Municipal PDFs and city fire pages (Pasadena, Bainbridge, Mercer Island, Santa Cruz County CERT, Manhattan Beach) dominate. That is a formatting and voice gap, not a missing topic.
- Evidence: Strong autocomplete: program, booklet, workbook, Washington State, video, worksheet. MYN is active in California cities in 2025–26.

**3. how to talk to neighbors about emergency preparedness**
- Intent: A. This is the emotional blocker, not the kit list.
- Page: Closest live pages are `/blog/first-10-steps-neighborhood-coordination`, `/blog/why-we-dont-say-prepper`, `/blog/who-are-your-three`. **New page needed** as a dedicated, practical script (what to say on a porch, what not to say).
- Competition: Low-medium. Reddit threads and a few calm guides (Fieldwise "without preaching," neighbour-to-neighbour PDFs). Prepper sites handle this poorly, which is the opening.
- Evidence: No tight Google autocomplete on the exact phrase, but repeated r/preppers demand: "Approaching Neighbors about Preparedness," "how to talk with/approach neighbors," "suburban middle class neighborhood where people keep to themselves," "Problems prepping in the Suburbs." Forum evidence is strong; treat volume as unknown.

**4. public safety power shutoff California**
- Intent: I / A / sometimes L ("today," "map," "near me").
- Page: `/scenarios/power-outage` and `/blog/flashlights-to-quiet-power-energy-buffer` are related but generic "7-day grid failure." **New page needed:** a PSPS-specific household-and-street plan (alerts, CRC sites, medical-device backup, garage doors, neighbor check-ins). Do not title it only "PSPS."
- Competition: High for official alerts and outage maps (SCE and other utilities). Medium/low for a neighborhood-coordination angle utilities do not write.
- Evidence: Full-phrase autocomplete is dense (California, event, today, watch, map, near me). Utility pages confirm events may last over 24 hours and that anyone in California can be affected. The acronym alone is not a usable query (meme collision).

**5. portable power station vs generator**
- Intent: C. Quiet indoor power vs. a gas generator, including apartments and PSPS.
- Page: `/blog/buyers-guide-backup-power-without-a-generator`, `/domain/energy_power`, `/product/solar-generators`. Update and target this query explicitly.
- Competition: High commercially (Bluetti, EcoFlow, review sites). The renter / no-carbon-monoxide / "the street can share a charger" angle is less saturated than camping or off-grid reviews.
- Evidence: Tight autocomplete (reddit, inverter, gas, solar, difference). BIFL threads ask for durable home backup. Towery and similar already connect the comparison to PSPS.

**6. how to store water in a small apartment**
- Intent: A / C. Space-constrained, often renter, sometimes high-rise (electric pumps fail when power fails).
- Page: `/blog/72-hour-water-plan-suburban-households` and `/blog/buyers-guide-water-storage-and-filtration` are suburban-household, not apartment. **New page needed** (or a clearly linked renter cut of the 72-hour plan).
- Competition: Medium. Some renter guides exist (About Preppers, ReadyCalm, Fieldwise). Ready.gov does not specialize. Prepper sites push barrels and basements.
- Evidence: Autocomplete: "emergency water storage apartment," "how to store water in a small apartment," "how to store drinkable water in apartment."

**7. wildfire smoke indoor air quality**
- Intent: I / A. Shelter-in-place during smoke, not evacuation.
- Page: `/scenarios/shelter-in-place` and `/scenarios/climate-failure` are adjacent (weeks at home; HVAC failure) but do not target smoke. **New page needed:** cleaner-air room for a suburban house or apartment.
- Competition: Official pages are strong (EPA "Create a Cleaner Air Room," AirNow, CDC, CDPH). Room to add a household-and-neighbors version: who has a HEPA unit, who is medically sensitive, when to go to a public cleaner-air space.
- Evidence: Autocomplete: indoor air quality, inside home, what to do, indoor cats. EPA/CDC guidance is the standard to cite, not to contradict.

**8. how to prepare for an atmospheric river**
- Intent: I / A. California / West Coast winter flood week, not generic "climate collapse."
- Page: `/scenarios/climate-failure` is HVAC, not flood. `/scenarios/water-disruption` and `/scenarios/evacuation` are adjacent. **New page needed.**
- Evidence: Autocomplete on the exact how-to, plus a dense "atmospheric river california" cluster (forecast, 2026, today, map, this week). Cal OES and DWR "Be Flood Ready" own the official slot; household-and-block practical copy is thinner.

**9. emergency kit for apartment**
- Intent: C / A.
- Page: No renter landing page. Closest: buyers guides, `/product/household-ready-plan`. **New page needed.**
- Competition: Medium. A growing set of renter guides; still far less than "72 hour kit" / "bug out bag."
- Evidence: Autocomplete on "emergency kit for apartment." Recurring constraints in those guides: no generator, no propane indoors, under-bed water, stairwells, elevators out.

**10. earthquake kit checklist California**
- Intent: C / A.
- Page: **New page needed**, and ideally a future `/scenarios/earthquake` (does not exist today). Link `window-status-cards`, `map-your-neighborhood-block`, water and medical guides, `/scenarios/shelter-in-place`.
- Competition: High for generic "earthquake kit" (Ready.gov, Red Cross, Cal OES-style lists). Opening: apartment / renter / block check-in after shaking, not a garage full of buckets.
- Evidence: Autocomplete: earthquake kit california, preparedness california, kit list/checklist california.

### Core household queries (real demand, official sites lead — differentiate)

**11. how much water to store for a family of 4**
- Intent: I / C.
- Page: `/blog/72-hour-water-plan-suburban-households`, `/blog/buyers-guide-water-storage-and-filtration`, `/domain/water_security`, `/scenarios/water-disruption`.
- Competition: High (CDC, Ready.gov, state EM). Win with household math, pets, suburban storage, and "what the block can share" — not a different gallon figure. CDC/Ready.gov: at least 1 gallon per person per day, 3 days minimum, 2 weeks preferred.

**12. boil water notice can I shower**
- Intent: I. A municipal, ordinary disruption. Prepper sites underserve it; utilities and health departments answer in FAQs.
- Page: `/scenarios/water-disruption` can absorb this, but **a short FAQ article is needed** so the exact questions can rank.
- Competition: Medium. State health FAQs (e.g. NY DOH-style boil-water FAQs) rank well. Little calm, shareable household content that also covers talking to neighbors and who has a kettle or camping stove outdoors.
- Evidence: Autocomplete: meaning, can I shower, near me, in my area, shower.

**13. flush toilet no running water**
- Intent: A. The sanitation question people actually type after a main break or earthquake.
- Page: `/blog/sanitation-the-quiet-axis`, `/domain/sanitation_hygiene`. Expand or add a practical how-to; the existing blog title is on-voice but not query-shaped.
- Competition: Medium (wikiHow, utility "be prepared" pages). Underserved in the neighborhood-resilience category.
- Evidence: Autocomplete: flush toilet without water, without water supply, no running water.

**14. how long will fridge keep cold in power outage**

- Competition: High official (USDA, FoodSafety.gov, CDC). Differentiate with "what the street does on hour five" and quiet backup power, not a rival safety number.
- Evidence: Dense autocomplete around fridge/freezer and "how long" / "how to keep cold."

**15. 30 day emergency food supply for family of 4**

- Page: `/blog/30-day-pantry-rotation-strategy`, `/blog/buyers-guide-food-storage-and-cooking`, `/scenarios/food-shortage`, `/domain/food_nutrition`.
- Competition: High commercially (bucket brands, food-storage blogs). Opening: food the household already eats, rotation, no doomsday menu.
- Evidence: Autocomplete: 30 day food supply for family of 4 / of 5, emergency food supply list.

**16. pantry staples for emergency**
- Intent: C / I. Softer entry than "30 day emergency food supply."
- Page: Same as #15. Good Pinterest/Instagram phrase.
- Evidence: Autocomplete: pantry staples for emergency, in case of emergency, pantry staples list.

**17. 15 minute evacuation list**
