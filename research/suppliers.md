# Terrarium supplier and maker list

Compiled 2026-11-11 (PT) for AQuantumArchitect/terrarium-storefront (https://store.terrariumproject.org). The order of steps is affiliates first, then direct suppliers, then Terrarium-made goods. This list is for the supplier step. It also flags which brands have their own affiliate programs, so they can go live before Amazon does.

**How to read this list:**
- Programs were checked by fetching each brand's homepage and its affiliate, wholesale or dealer pages on 2026-11-11.
- "(per page)" means the brand's own page states the terms. "(third-party)" or "unverified" means the terms came from an aggregator listing or a search result.
- No prices are recorded anywhere. Commission figures are what each page said on 2026-11-11 and can change.
- "None found on site" means no affiliate or wholesale link appeared on the homepage or at the usual URL paths. It does not prove the brand has no program.
- No one was contacted and no accounts were created.
- ShareASale finished merging into Awin on 2025-08-15, so ShareASale programs now live on Awin.

**Codes:**
- Axes: WS water, EP energy, FN food, SA sanitation, HM health, SS security, CI communication, TM transport, TL tools, SH shelter, GC governance, CR culture.
- Size: indie (small or independent), mid, or major.
- Alignment: 1-5. A 5 is mission-aligned, sells what a Terrarium concept already calls for, and has a usable program.

**Totals:**
- 178 brands in the table.
- 118 of them already appear somewhere in the Terrarium repos.
- 69 have an affiliate program confirmed on their own page or a program page.
- 46 have an open wholesale, dealer or reseller path.
- Many others have a dealer locator or an unclear status; see each row.

**Notes on the shortlist:**
- Governance and culture have few real suppliers. Most of the governance fit is software or nonprofits (Loomio, myTurn, Little Free Library), which are partners rather than suppliers.
- White Duck is the only brand found that names dropshipping on its retail-partner page. Ask any supplier you approach whether it dropships before Terrarium-made or stocked goods are possible.
- Steer clear for now:
  - Berkey: the EPA stop-sale order on Black Berkey elements was upheld on 2026-09-18.
  - Sun Oven: the maker paused operations in 2025 and no new ovens are for sale. GoSun is the working alternative.
  - Rad Power Bikes: it filed Chapter 11 in Dec 2025, and Life EV bought the brand in 2026.
  - Preppi: its site returned an error on 2026-11-11.
- Two tone checks: My Patriot Supply's political branding is a mismatch with Terrarium's voice, and BTECH/Baofeng radios have a mixed reputation.

## Gaps and next steps

- **Food is over-represented** (59 brands) because seeds and storage are deep niches. The thin axes are governance (4) and security (7). For security, the next sweep should look at fire and CO detection (Kidde, First Alert), fire extinguishers, and seismic and flood hardening. For governance, it should look at civic and neighborhood-kit makers.
- **Unverified items to recheck in a browser** (the site blocked the fetch or didn't respond): Reliance, Berkey (berkeywater.com), My Patriot Supply, Be Prepared, Uncle Jim's, Country Living, Bike Friday, Solo Stove, Franklin, Peaceable Kingdom, Thule, Allwood and Preppi.
- **Programs found only on aggregator sites:** Mountain House, Katadyn, Johnny's, Hoss Tools, Lectric, Rad Power, Bambu Lab and iFixit. Confirm on the brand's own page before applying.
- **Prerequisite for most affiliate networks** (Impact, AvantLink, Awin, CJ, Pepperjam/Ascend, Partnerize): an approved publisher account for the store. That is a Luke action, since it involves a tax form and payout details. Shopify Collabs, UpPromote, GoAffPro and in-house programs usually approve per brand.
- **Wholesale and dealer applications usually need a resale certificate and a business entity.** Terrarium has no legal vehicle yet, so affiliate programs are the realistic first move. Wholesale waits for the entity or fiscal sponsor.
