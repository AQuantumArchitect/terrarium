# Municipal and civic partners for Terrarium

**Research date:** Wednesday, November 11, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles)  
**Scope:** Municipal and civic partners only (emergency management, CERT, Listos, Map Your Neighborhood–style programs, neighborhood councils, fire safe councils, resilience hubs, libraries, VOAD, Red Cross, school preparedness, and national models worth copying). A separate pass covers eco-villages and intentional communities.  
**Focus region first:** Ventura County and neighboring cities and counties (Camarillo, Oxnard, Thousand Oaks, Simi Valley, Ventura, Moorpark, Santa Barbara County, Los Angeles County west side), then notable California and national models.  
**Method:** Checked GitHub (`AQuantumArchitect/terrarium`, `terrarium-storefront`, `terrarium-ecom`) and `/home/box` notes first, then official public websites. Facts below are taken from those pages or from official PDFs linked from them. Nothing here was invented. No one was contacted. Luke sends any outreach himself.  
**Location rule:** This memo does not name or imply Luke’s exact city or address. The focus region is stated because it is the project’s civic theater, not a residence claim.

## Constraints and how to use this list

- Public contact routes only: official websites, contact forms, and public or program emails published on those sites. Personal cell numbers that appear on some official pages (for example the VCFD CERT coordinator cell on [fire.venturacounty.gov/cert](https://fire.venturacounty.gov/cert/)) are **omitted on purpose**.
- Named staff work emails are included only when a city or county published them as the program contact.
- Class dates go stale quickly. Several 2026 CERT series published earlier this year had already ended by the date of this research. Always check the official page before mentioning a schedule.
- Terrarium is not an Amazon Associate or Etsy affiliate, has no legal nonprofit vehicle yet, and should not claim FEMA, Cal OES, or city endorsement. The first offer to municipal partners should be the free 12-domain framework and neighbor-coordination tools, not the store.
- Fit scores (1–5) are judgment about partnership usefulness for Terrarium (overlap with the Suburban Gap thesis, a clear public door, and a realistic near-term ask). They are not rankings of the programs themselves.

## Top 15 warm leads

These are the first conversations worth having, in roughly this order. All are in or immediately adjacent to the focus region, have a public door, and have a clear Terrarium ask that does not require a 501(c)(3) on day one.

1. **Ready Ventura County / Ventura County Sheriff’s Office of Emergency Services** — The county’s public preparedness brand already invites organizations to “plug ready.venturacounty.gov” on their websites and newsletters, and its four steps (stay informed, make a plan, build a kit, get involved) line up with Terrarium’s household-plus-neighbors frame. Ask: a link exchange and a briefing on the 12-domain checklist as a neighbor-coordination overlay, not a competing alert system. Contact: [ready.venturacounty.gov](https://ready.venturacounty.gov/) and the OES page at [sheriff.venturacounty.gov/patrol-services/office-of-emergency-services](https://sheriff.venturacounty.gov/patrol-services/office-of-emergency-services/). Fit: 5.

2. **Ventura County Fire Department CERT program** — Countywide coordinator of free ~24-hour CERT classes with city-level teams in Camarillo, Oxnard, Thousand Oaks, Simi Valley, Ventura, Moorpark, and others, plus two high-school teams. Terrarium already publishes CERT as civic training, not a “bunker club.” Ask: be listed as a post-class household/neighborhood practice resource; offer a 12-domain workshop for graduates. Contact: [fire.venturacounty.gov/cert](https://fire.venturacounty.gov/cert/) or `vcfd@venturacounty.gov`. Fit: 5.

3. **Ventura County VOAD (VC-VOAD)** — Collaborative of nearly fifty member organizations; runs Ventura County Prepares and already points the public to VCFD CERT and Listos California. Natural home for a free framework that fills the “coordination between neighbors” gap. Ask: membership or a resource listing, and a short briefing for members. Contact: [vcvoad.org/contact](https://www.vcvoad.org/contact), `vcvoad@gmail.com`. Fit: 5.

4. **City of Thousand Oaks CERT, plus the city’s planned library resilience hub** — Active city CERT page, and the Adaptation and Resilience strategy is already planning “an emergency cooling and evacuation center at the Grant R. Brimhall Library” and resilience hubs at city facilities (CEAP measure AR2.1). This is the closest local match to the USDN / SGC hub model. Ask: CERT workshop plus a conversation with the climate/resilience staff about the library hub. Contact: [toaks.gov/cert](https://www.toaks.gov/cert) (`pweinstein@toaks.gov`); [toaks.gov/adaptation-resilience-ceap](https://toaks.gov/adaptation-resilience-ceap) or `city@toaks.gov`. Fit: 5.

5. **City of Camarillo CERT** — City-hosted, VCFD-taught, with a public coordinator email and volunteer portal. Strong neighborhood-scale training door in the county’s geographic center. Ask: co-host or supply a 12-domain checklist session after a CERT cycle. Contact: [cityofcamarillo.org CERT registration](https://www.cityofcamarillo.org/departments/police/cert_registration.php), `hzahrt@cityofcamarillo.org`. Fit: 5.

6. **City of Oxnard CERT / Emergency Services** — Program email (`cert@oxnard.org`), city EOC as a class site, and the largest city in the county. Pairs well with MICOP’s indigenous-language preparedness work in the same city. Ask: resource listing and a bilingual workshop conversation (Terrarium should not invent translation capacity; partner with MICOP or Listos materials). Contact: [VCFD CERT Oxnard](https://volunteer.vcfd.org/agency/detail/?agency_id=119816), `cert@oxnard.org`. Fit: 5.

7. **City of Ventura Emergency Services / CERT** — City CERT page (sometimes offered with Santa Paula). Public program contact is the Emergency Services Manager. Ask: same workshop/checklist offer; check the page for the next cycle before writing (none were scheduled on the date of this research). Contact: [cityofventura.ca.gov/CERT](https://cityofventura.ca.gov/CERT), `mdouglass@cityofventura.ca.gov`. Fit: 4.

8. **City of Simi Valley CERT and Disaster Service Worker Volunteers** — City CERT program, and Ready Ventura County’s Get Involved page also highlights Simi Valley’s Disaster Service Worker Volunteer team for preparedness, response, and recovery. Ask: checklist workshop for CERT graduates and DSW volunteers. Contact: [simivalley.org CERT](https://www.simivalley.org/departments/police-department/emergency-services/community-emergency-response-team-cert) and [ready.venturacounty.gov/get-involved](https://ready.venturacounty.gov/get-involved/). Fit: 4.

9. **City of Moorpark Emergency Management** — Small-city OEM with a dedicated program email; steers residents into the Thousand Oaks and Simi CERT cycles. A good test of a “use the 12-domain checklist in a city that does not run its own class” ask. Contact: [moorparkca.gov/148/CERT-Training](https://www.moorparkca.gov/148/CERT-Training), `CERT@moorparkca.gov`. Fit: 4.

10. **American Red Cross Pacific Coast Chapter** — Chapter office is in Camarillo and serves Ventura, Santa Barbara, and San Luis Obispo Counties (~1.5 million people). First aid, CPR, and disaster response already sit next to Terrarium’s health and civic concepts. Ask: class cross-referral and a preparedness talk, not a fundraising pitch. Contact: [Red Cross Pacific Coast Chapter](https://www.redcross.org/local/california/central-california/about-us/locations/pacific-coast.html); 836 Calle Plano, Camarillo; chapter phone (805) 987-1514 or 1-800-RED-CROSS. Fit: 4.

11. **United Way of Ventura County / Volunteer Ventura County** — VOAD member, 2-1-1 partner, Disaster Spontaneous Volunteer registration, and the public portal that already lists CERT classes. Ask: list Terrarium’s free frameworks on Volunteer Ventura County and in the United We Prepare guide if they update it. Contact: [vcunitedway.org/disaster-relief](https://vcunitedway.org/disaster-relief/), `info@vcunitedway.org`; volunteer portal [volunteerventuracounty.org](https://www.volunteerventuracounty.org/). Fit: 4.

12. **Ventura Regional Fire Safe Council** — County’s regional wildfire-preparedness nonprofit; free home wildfire and hardening assessments; already republishes city CERT schedules. The 12-domain checklist is a natural complement to defensible-space work (neighbors, water, communications, go-bags). Ask: resource link and a neighborhood workshop alongside a Firewise or assessment event. Contact: [venturafiresafe.org/contact](https://venturafiresafe.org/contact/), `info@venturafiresafe.org`. Fit: 4.

13. **Mixteco/Indígena Community Organizing Project (MICOP)** — Oxnard-headquartered, with a documented Listos California Target Grant for culturally and linguistically competent disaster preparedness in Ventura, Santa Barbara, and San Luis Obispo Counties (outreach in Mixteco, Zapoteco, and Purépecha, plus Radio Indígena). The highest-fit equity partner in the focus region. Ask: a conversation about whether Terrarium’s frameworks can be useful in their existing sessions; do not lead with the store, and do not claim language capacity Terrarium does not have. Contact: [mixteco.org/contact](https://mixteco.org/contact/), `communications@mixteco.org` or `events@mixteco.org`. Fit: 4.

14. **Listos California** — Statewide “Prepare. Respond. Recover. Together.” campaign with multilingual Disaster Ready Guides, a local-alert signup helper, and a **neighborhood block-party toolkit**. This is the state-level program named in Luke’s earlier grant notes, and it is distinct from Santa Barbara’s local LISTOS – Aware & Prepare program (see the full table). Ask: use and credit the block-party toolkit; later, if a legal vehicle exists, explore becoming a local education partner. Contact: [listoscalifornia.org](https://www.listoscalifornia.org/), `hello@listoscalifornia.org`. Fit: 4.

15. **Ventura County Library cooling-center network, with Thousand Oaks’ Brimhall Library as the flagship civic-hub conversation** — Multiple Ventura County Library branches are designated cooling centers during regular hours. Combined with Thousand Oaks’ planned emergency cooling and evacuation center at Grant R. Brimhall Library, this is the practical local start on resilience hubs. Ask: a quiet-resilience / 12-domain display or workshop during heat or wildfire season, not a new building proposal. Contact: [library.venturacounty.gov/library-service/cooling-centers](https://www.library.venturacounty.gov/library-service/cooling-centers); Thousand Oaks via `city@toaks.gov` and the adaptation page above. Fit: 4.

## Gaps, caveats, and things not found

- **No official Ventura County “Map Your Neighborhood” administrator was found.** A 2021 Camarillo Acorn preparedness article mentioned MYN as a program “used by some cities,” but that is journalism, not a city program page. Until a city or the county publishes an MYN page, Terrarium should describe neighbor mapping as a practice and point to RYLAN, the LA County 5-Step kit, or Washington’s official MYN, rather than claiming a local MYN office exists.
- **No LA-style neighborhood council system was found in Ventura County cities.** Partnerships at the block level will more often run through CERT teams, Firewise/HOA lists (especially Oak Park), libraries, VOAD members, and city OEM newsletters.
- **Sheriff OES “Local Emergency Management Offices” links** on the official OES page, as retrieved, listed only Camarillo, Ojai, San Buenaventura, and Thousand Oaks (the last appeared with a typo, “City of San Thousand Oaks”). Other cities clearly have emergency-services or CERT functions (Oxnard, Simi Valley, Moorpark, and others). Use each city’s own page rather than treating that short list as complete.
- **Class schedules in 2026 move fast.** Ventura’s city page had no class scheduled on this research date; several spring and summer 2026 series had already ended. Volunteer-portal flyers are useful hints, not sources of record.
- **Listos naming collision.** Listos California (listoscalifornia.org) is the statewide campaign. LISTOS – Aware & Prepare (listos.org) is a Santa Barbara–based training program. Both are real; they are not the same organization.
- **Legal vehicle.** Formal MOUs, VOAD membership in Terrarium’s own name, and SGC or Listos grant roles will likely need a fiscal sponsor or nonprofit. Informal link exchanges and workshop offers can be made sooner, in Luke’s name, without overclaiming institutional status.
- **UCSB and expired BRIC grant research** belong primarily on the grants pass. They were not treated as municipal operating partners here.
- **Not contacted, not endorsed.** Appearance on this list is not an endorsement of Terrarium by the organization, and no outreach was sent.
